userspace.party

A small Rust ecosystem for exploring userspace from the bottom up: syscalls, loaders, runtimes, kernel boundaries and the pieces that make a process feel at home without taking libc for granted.

The hub and its eight submodules move together as one versioned ecosystem while remaining useful as standalone repositories.

userspace_hub The workspace and integration point.
userspace The core userspace building blocks.
ample Shared low-level support for the ecosystem.
kernelspace The other side of the userspace boundary.
userspace_build Build-time machinery and integration.
webspace Web-facing experiments in the same family.
twins Paired abstractions and experiments.
humans Human-facing pieces of the stack.
computers Computer-facing pieces of the stack.

userspace.party is the public front door for the rust_userspace ecosystem: deliberately small, inspectable and close to the machine. The source stays on GitHub, releases stay on crates.io, and this site gives the family a place to meet.
